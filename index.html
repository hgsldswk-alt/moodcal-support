帮助与隐私

记录、补记与备份

打开首页点一档即保存。点日期可补记、回看和编辑当天记录。设置 → 导出历史可保存 CSV 和完整备份；换机后选择「恢复备份」，确认摘要即可合并，冲突时保留本机记录。

试用与购买

30 天试用从首次打开起算，结束不自动扣费。高级功能可一次买断；设置里可以恢复购买。到期仍可记录、回看、编辑与完整备份。

隐私说明

我们不收集心情、备注或个人信息。没有注册、广告、分析和自建服务器。日记数据库只在本机且排除自动 iCloud 备份。通知由你自行开启，购买与恢复由 App Store 处理。

分享卡默认隐藏日期，且不包含备注；下载码可关闭。分享、导出或复制时由你决定保存和发送位置。可在 App 内删除记录；卸载会移除本机日记，不会删除你已导出的副本。

Help and privacy

Log, backfill and back up

Tap one of five moods to save it. Select a date to add or edit an entry. Settings → Export history saves a CSV and full backup. On a new device, choose Restore backup and confirm the preview. If entries conflict, the device keeps its local version.

Trial and purchases

The 30-day trial starts when you first open the app and never charges automatically. Pro is a one-time purchase; you can restore it in Settings. Logging, viewing, editing and full backups remain available after the trial.

Privacy

We do not collect moods, notes or personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or servers operated by us. Your journal database is stored on the device and excluded from automatic iCloud backups. Reminders are opt-in, and the App Store handles purchases and restoration.

Share cards hide dates by default and never include notes. You can turn off the download code. You choose where to save or send exports. You can delete entries in the app; uninstalling removes the local journal, not copies you already exported.
